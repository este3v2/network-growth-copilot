Before you start

What you need.

Nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for, other than Claude Code itself.

People and companies

The unit is a person. Companies hang off people.

You often want a relationship with a company, not only with one individual. This is how the kit handles that today, and where it stops.

QuestionHow it works today
Is a company a record?No. There is no company file. A company is the company field on each person who works there.
Can I see everyone at one company?Yes, by filtering or sorting on company in the board view, or by asking "who do I know at Northwind?".
Does network-next rank companies?No. It ranks people. Organisations are kept outside the ranking on purpose.
How do I track target companies or startups?Keep a separate research note outside the contact folder: a map of companies, in groups, with the warm contacts already inside each one linked to their person file.
When does a company become outreach?Only when you have a named person and a real hook. A company on the map alone never produces a message.

Example from my own use: a note mapping AI companies in Australia, split into product companies and consulting firms, with "already warm" people listed first. It is a map, not an outreach queue.

What it keeps to

Three rules that don't bend.

You sendNothing goes out automatically. Ever.
You approveNo file changes without you pasting it in.
A real hook, or silenceIf a file has nothing specific, it says so and writes nothing generic.

Claude Code doesn't take audio, so "say it" means dictate with your phone or a voice memo and paste the text. Obsidian is optional. It only adds a board view.
