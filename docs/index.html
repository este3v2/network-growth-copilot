What to say

Three skills. Say it or type it.

dictate on your phone, paste the text type it
1 · Capture

meeting-capture

Turns a conversation into an entry on that person's file.

"Just met Priya at the Data Summit. She runs analytics at Northwind and her team rebuilds the same report every week. I said I'd send the case study."
Capture a meeting with Marco. [paste notes]

You get an entry to paste. It never edits an existing file.

2 · Rank

network-next

Says who deserves attention now, and why.

"Who should I follow up with this week?"
Network next, phone overview

Three actions with evidence, or 15 lines to scan. Read-only.

3 · Draft

network-outreach

Writes one message to one person, in three tones.

"Draft a LinkedIn message to Priya."
Email Marco, thoughtful tone

Drafts only. You send, then paste the log line back.

The rhythm

Ten minutes, three moments.

After each meetingDictate what happened. Paste the entry it hands back.
Once a weekAsk who to follow up with. Pick one or two.
When you pick someoneAsk for a draft. Edit, send, paste the log line.
What it keeps to

Three rules that don't bend.

You sendNothing goes out automatically. Ever.
You approveNo file changes without you pasting it in.
A real hook, or silenceIf a file has nothing specific, it says so and writes nothing generic.

Claude Code doesn't take audio, so "say it" means dictate with your phone or a voice memo and paste the text. Obsidian is optional. It only adds a board view.
